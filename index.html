# Privacy Policy for Commission Count **Effective Date:** March 30, 2025 **Commission Count** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Commission Count** mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play's Developer Program Policies and Data Safety requirements. --- ## 1. Information Collection and Use To provide and improve our App, we integrate third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or analyze app performance. ### A. Firebase Analytics We use **Google Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymous usage data, feature interaction metrics (such as calculation events), and general app engagement statistics. This helps us understand how users interact with the App so we can improve its functionality and user experience. * **Data Collected:** Anonymous app usage data, interaction events, device information, operating system version, and general analytics. ### B. Firebase Crashlytics We use **Google Firebase Crashlytics** to collect diagnostic data and crash reports when the App encounters an unexpected error or exception. This allows us to quickly identify, troubleshoot, and fix bugs. * **Data Collected:** Crash logs, stack traces, device state information at the time of the crash, and non-fatal error reports. --- ## 2. Permissions Required The App requests the following minimal permissions: * `INTERNET`: Required to communicate with Firebase servers for analytics reporting and crash diagnostics. * `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to check network connectivity status before sending diagnostic or analytics data. --- ## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. Data collected via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics is processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). --- ## 4. Data Security The security of your data is important to us. All network communications between the App and Firebase servers are encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS protocols. --- ## 5. Children's Privacy Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. --- ## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
